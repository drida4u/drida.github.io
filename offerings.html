Meditation

Stillness Begins Within

Drida's meditation is not about clearing the mind — it's about gently meeting it. Smitha Haridas leads a blend of ancient practices, live, every day:

  • Chakra Meditation — energy centre awareness and activation
  • Breathwork (Pranayama) — calming the nervous system through conscious breath
  • Guided Visualisation — building inner clarity and positive self-image
  • Ho'oponopono — Hawaiian practice of forgiveness and healing
  • Affirmations — building a grounded sense of self-worth
